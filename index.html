About me

I'm a postdoctoral fellow at the Mathematics Institute of the University of Warwick.  Some of my interests include: applications of dynamical systems to metric number theory and flat surfaces; running; bread; and trees.

Academic positions

  • Postdoctoral Fellow.  Mathematics Institute, University of Warwick (November 2026 – Present).
    • Funded by Mark Pollicott's EPSRC Open Fellowship, Computational Ergodic Theory, Hyperbolic Geometry and Spectral Theory.
  • Postdoctoral Fellow.  Institut de Recherche en Informatique Fondamentale (IRIF), Université Paris Cité (November 2025 – October 2026).
    • Funded by Valérie Berthé and Wolfgang Steiner's ANR and FWF project, SymDynAr.
  • Early Career Researcher.  Simons Semester: Continued Fractions, Fractals, Ergodic Theory and Dynamics.  Institute of Mathematics of the Polish Academy of Sciences (May 2026).
