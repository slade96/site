About me

I'm a research fellow at the Mathematics Institute of the University of Warwick.  My interests include applications of dynamical systems to metric number theory and flat surfaces.

Employment

  • Research Fellow.  Mathematics Institute, University of Warwick (November 2026 – Present).
    • With Mark Pollicott.
  • Postdoctoral Fellow.  Institut de Recherche en Informatique Fondamentale (IRIF), Université Paris Cité (November 2025 – October 2026).
    • With Valérie Berthé and Wolfgang Steiner.
  • Early Career Researcher.  Simons Semester: Continued Fractions, Fractals, Ergodic Theory and Dynamics.  Institute of Mathematics of the Polish Academy of Sciences (May 2026).
